Project

What the system does

The project helps replace manual fitness-test review with a repeatable AI-assisted workflow. Athletes upload exercise videos, and the backend processes movement quality, repetitions, benchmarks, and authenticity signals.

01

Analyze movement

Detect body landmarks, joint angles, range of motion, and exercise phases.

02

Score performance

Calculate reps, form score, grade, fitness level, and useful feedback.

03

Compare candidates

Show leaderboards, registered counts, completion counts, flags, and shortlists.
