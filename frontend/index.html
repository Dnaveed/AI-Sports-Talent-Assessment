About

Objective fitness evaluation, powered by AI

Traditional fitness assessments rely on manual observation — inconsistent, slow, and hard to scale. This system replaces subjective evaluation with computer vision-based pose estimation that delivers consistent, data-driven results for every athlete, every time. Recruitment bodies get reliable performance data to make confident selection decisions.

01

Pose-Based Movement Analysis

MediaPipe extracts 33 body landmarks per frame to detect joint angles, range of motion, and exercise phases with precision.

02

Automated Performance Scoring

Each submission is scored on rep count, form quality, fitness level, and percentile — with an A–F grade and actionable feedback.

03

Recruitment-Ready Rankings

Automated leaderboards rank candidates by weighted performance metrics, enabling authorities to shortlist talent efficiently.

Workflow

From assessment creation to candidate selection

A structured end-to-end pipeline that handles scheduling, video submission, AI analysis, and recruitment decisions.

01Schedule AssessmentRecruitment authority defines exercises, weights, date, time, and participant limits.
02Athlete RegistrationAthletes browse open assessments, register, and receive notifications for upcoming evaluations.
03Video SubmissionAthletes record and upload exercise videos through the platform for AI processing.
04AI EvaluationPose estimation engine counts reps, scores form, detects anomalies, and generates performance metrics.
05Ranking & SelectionAuthority reviews ranked leaderboard, shortlists candidates, and exports results as CSV or PDF.
Features

Everything needed for modern fitness recruitment

Built around the full evaluation lifecycle — from video upload to final candidate selection.

P

Pose Estimation & Rep Counting Tracks push-ups, squats, sit-ups, lunges, jumping jacks, and vertical jumps using MediaPipe's 33-point landmark model.

F

Form Scoring & Grading 0–100 form score with A–F grade, phase-level breakdown, benchmark comparison, and improvement recommendations.

I

Integrity & Cheat Detection Flags low-confidence submissions, suspicious movement patterns, and partial reps for authority review.

R

Recruitment Leaderboards Weighted ranking across multiple exercises with export to CSV/PDF for official record-keeping and selection.

D

Athlete Progress Dashboard Historical results, performance trends, achievement badges, personal goals, and fitness level tracking over time.

A

Analytics & Reporting Platform-wide statistics, AI quality metrics, participation rates, and authority-level assessment analytics.
